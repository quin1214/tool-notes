Start a newsletter for $0 on beehiiv: what's free, and the catches

Prices and terms checked on beehiiv.com on October 26, 2026. They change, so please confirm on beehiiv's pricing page before you decide.

beehiiv is a platform for writing and sending an email newsletter, with a website for your posts. Its Free plan covers up to 2,500 subscribers with unlimited sends and no credit card. Below is what you get, what it leaves out, and when you'd need to pay, based on what beehiiv itself publishes.

1. "Will I have to pay to get started?"

No. There is a free plan with no credit card required.

The Free plan covers up to 2,500 subscribers, with unlimited email sends. You can stay on it as long as you like. Paid plans add features; they are not required just to send a newsletter.

2. "What do the paid plans actually cost?"

The price depends on how many subscribers you have. At 1,000 subscribers, beehiiv lists:

Prices go up as your list grows, so check the slider on the pricing page for your own number. Yearly billing saves up to 20%.

3. "Is the free trial a trap?"

It does not ask for a card, and if you do nothing, you simply stay on the Free plan.

According to beehiiv's help center, the trial gives you 14 days of Pro features with no credit card. If you don't upgrade, your content and subscribers stay, and the Pro features turn off.

One thing to know: some features are held back during the trial, including custom domains, paid subscriptions, multiple publications and extra team seats. So the trial is good for testing the editor, automations and analytics, but not for launching a paid newsletter on your own domain.

4. "Does beehiiv take a cut of what I earn?"

beehiiv says it takes 0% of paid subscription and digital product revenue. Stripe's standard payment fee (2.9% + $0.30 per transaction) still applies. Note that paid subscriptions are only available on Lite and above, not on the Free plan.

5. "What are the catches on the Free plan?"

Being honest about the limits:

  • Custom domains are not included on Free. They start on Lite.
  • beehiiv branding stays on Free and Lite. Removing it requires Pro.
  • Free has 1 team seat and a 30-day analytics lookback window.
  • Paid subscriptions, the ad network, A/B testing and custom HTML are paid-plan features.

If you only want to send a simple update to a small list, the Free plan may be all you need, and you may never pay anything.

6. "What happens if I outgrow the plan?"

You can upgrade at any time. The Free plan stops at 2,500 subscribers, so a growing list will eventually mean a paid plan. It is worth looking at the price for your expected list size now, so it is not a surprise later.
