Moving your podcast to Transistor? Price, limits and switching, before you start

Prices and terms checked on transistor.fm on October 26, 2026. They change, so please confirm on Transistor's pricing page before you decide.

Transistor is a podcast hosting service: you upload episodes, and it publishes your show's feed to Apple Podcasts, Spotify and other apps. There's no free plan, but there are no overage fees either, and you can bring an existing show over by RSS. Below is what to check first, whether you're switching hosts or starting a new show, based on what Transistor itself publishes.

1. "Is there a free plan?"

No. There is a 14-day free trial, then you pay for a plan.

Transistor's terms say the trial automatically converts into the paid plan unless you cancel before it ends. If you are only testing, put a reminder in your calendar for day 13. The pages I checked do not say whether a card is asked for at signup, so look for that on the signup screen.

Yearly billing is 12 months for the price of 10. Every plan includes unlimited podcasts and unlimited team members, so one account can hold several shows. Note that the download limit is shared across all of them. Transcription is an add-on ($5/month for 5 hours, $20/month for 20 hours).

3. "What happens if I go over my download limit?"

Your audio keeps playing. There are no surprise overage fees.

Transistor's terms say it will not charge overage fees and will not change your plan without your consent. Instead, it contacts you and asks you to move to a plan that fits. If you stay over the limit for 30 days after being notified, it may lock the account: you can't publish new episodes until you upgrade, but existing episodes keep being delivered to listeners.

4. "Can I move my existing podcast without losing listeners?"

Transistor's help docs describe importing a show from its RSS feed. You confirm ownership by email, then set a redirect at your old host so existing subscribers follow the show to the new feed.

Two things to know before you start: your old analytics don't carry over, so export them first, and the old feed must not be locked.

5. "Do I own my content?"

Yes. Transistor's FAQ says you keep ownership of everything you upload. There's no limit on the number of episodes; single audio files can be up to 2 GB and video files up to 30 GB.

6. "Which plan do I actually need?"

Check your current monthly downloads (or your best guess for a new show). If it's under 20,000, Starter covers it, and you can upgrade later from your account. The higher plans add dynamic ads, auto-posting to YouTube, more private podcast subscribers, and (on Business) removal of Transistor branding. If you don't need those, there's no reason to start higher.
