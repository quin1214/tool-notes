Sunsama: is a $17/month daily planner worth it for you?

Prices and terms checked on sunsama.com on October 26, 2026. They change, so please confirm on Sunsama's pricing page before you decide.

Sunsama is a daily planner: each morning you pull tasks from your calendar, email and work tools into one list, decide what fits in the day, and block time for it. It has no free plan, but the 14-day trial needs no card. Below is how to judge it before you pay anything, based on what Sunsama itself publishes.

1. "Isn't this expensive for a to-do app?"

It's paid only. There is no free plan.

The Pro plan is $17/month billed yearly, or $22/month billed monthly (USD). Sunsama says openly that it doesn't offer a free plan, lifetime deals or holiday sales. Whether it's worth it depends on whether the daily planning routine actually helps you. That's what the trial is for.

2. "Will the trial charge me if I forget to cancel?"

No card is asked for during the trial.

The trial is 14 days with all features unlocked and no credit card required. Sunsama says it only asks for a card after the trial, if you decide to keep going. So forgetting about it costs nothing.

3. "Will it work with the tools I already use?"

Sunsama lists integrations with Google Calendar, Outlook Calendar, Gmail, Outlook, Slack, Microsoft Teams, Notion, Asana, Trello, ClickUp, Jira, Linear, GitHub, Todoist, Microsoft To-Do, Google Tasks, Monday.com, Toggl and Zapier, among others. Apps are available for iOS/iPadOS, Android, macOS, Windows and Linux. During the trial, connect the two or three tools you use most and see whether the daily list feels useful.

4. "Can I cancel easily?"

Sunsama says you can cancel at any time without contacting support, and you keep access until the end of the billing period you've paid for. The pricing page doesn't promise refunds for the unused part of a period, so if you're unsure, start with monthly billing rather than yearly.

5. "What if I want to use it with my team?"

Pricing is per person. Each person you add to your workspace is added to your bill, prorated for the first month. Companies that need SSO, SAML or custom security requirements are directed to an Enterprise plan.

6. "Who probably doesn't need it?"

If a simple checklist already works for you, a free to-do app may be enough. Sunsama is built around a daily planning habit and connecting many work tools. If you don't want that routine, the price is hard to justify.
