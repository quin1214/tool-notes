Want to start a newsletter without paying up front?
Here's what beehiiv lets you do for $0, and where it stops.

"Do I have to pay to start?"
No. The Free plan covers up to 2,500 subscribers with unlimited email sends. No credit card, and you can stay on it as long as you like.

"Is the 14-day trial a trap?"
The Pro trial needs no card. If you don't upgrade, you stay on Free and keep your posts and subscribers.

"So what's the catch?"
No custom domain, beehiiv branding stays on, and paid subscriptions need a paid plan. Lite starts at $49/month billed yearly (at 1,000 subscribers).
