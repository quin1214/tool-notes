## 本文（約120語。続きのピンや説明に使う）

Moving your podcast, or starting one, and worried about the fine print?
Three things to know before you try Transistor.

"Is there a free plan?"
No. You get a 14-day trial, and it turns into a paid plan unless you cancel. Plans start at $19/month (Starter, 20,000 downloads a month).

"What if my show grows past the limit?"
Your episodes keep playing, with no overage fees. Transistor asks you to upgrade; new uploads are locked only if you stay over for 30 days after being told.

"Will I lose my listeners if I switch?"
You import from your RSS feed and redirect the old one, so subscribers follow. Old analytics don't transfer, so export them first.
